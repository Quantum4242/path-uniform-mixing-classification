# G. Convex interior, timing noise and smooth time densities

The historical triple-prime note proves an explicit real cosine-moment margin conditional on a stronger phase law: if same-parity moments are −1/(N−1) and opposite-parity moments are zero, then B₁(y,1/(2N)) lies in the moment convex hull interior. The elementary Haar density 1+2∑ x_ω cos(ωt) is nonnegative when ∑|x_ω|≤1/2 on the phase group, and yields the zero-centred moment ball. Convex mixing gives the margin at y.

The same note treats known symmetric independent timing noise supported on [−1/(2N),1/(2N)] for odd q (even N), with the protocol reoptimized for that noise. Taking a smooth symmetric compactly supported bump in this range and convolving a suitably reweighted finite readout law yields a smooth compactly supported exact time density. Choose the nominal times sufficiently far from zero if nonnegative actual times are required. The original note recognizes prior smooth/continuous existence in already known cases; do not assert blanket novelty.

The later third audit and main paper give a stronger *conditional on the classification construction* uniform Haar floor ν≥h/100, complex moment ℓ¹ radius 1/200, and compensation for known independent noise with standard deviation at most 1/(25√N). The centered characteristic function satisfies |k_ω−1|≤ω²σ²/2≤8σ². The noise need not be symmetric in the later result. The mean is handled by time translation; support on nonnegative actual times for arbitrary unbounded noise is not claimed. All times use the dimensionless adjacency Hamiltonian. These are existence results for a redesigned distribution, not guarantees of a fixed schedule under unknown noise, nor experimental demonstrations.
