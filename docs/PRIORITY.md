The proposed novelty is the positive direction for all previously unclassified path moduli using the two low-degree polynomials, their short-relation correction and the quantitative floor. It is not the original question, necessary conditions, classical number theory, Fourier orthogonality, or classical convex geometry. The earlier project-specific P8/P19 results are antecedent work in the same research thread; the P19 Lean certificate is historical and not a proof dependency of the new general argument. The previous repository contents were not updated or re-published in this release. The cited works are linked rather than redistributed.
