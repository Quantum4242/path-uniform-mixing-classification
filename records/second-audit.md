# Second audit: results and limitations

## 1. Improvements
The candidate theorem is unchanged: for q>=3, P_(q−1) admits uniform average mixing iff 15 and 21 do not divide q, and either 6 does not divide q or q=6. The negative direction is Kang's prior result. This round improves the proof of the positive direction, not the claimed classification itself.

The global localization lemma in the unified proof makes explicit why signed minimal-support decomposition, projective lifting, and reflection can be used even if the chosen minimal partition itself is not inversion invariant. The order-35 symmetric and antisymmetric tables now follow by elementary absolute-value inequalities, and the order-385 exceptional central slice is excluded analytically. The previous large bounded enumeration remains a cross-check, not a premise.

## 2. Fourteen finite exceptions no longer require probability certificates
For each of thirteen even moduli the original low-degree Fourier density works when the actual signed Fourier corrections are computed rather than the coarse uniform error bound. All 26 parity groups have strictly positive unnormalized lower bounds (exact fractions in exact-density-margins.txt). The complete integer kernel list was independently obtained by row reduction of cyclotomic power-basis vectors and enumerating all free integer coordinates of L1 norm <=10. There were 30,094,218 free-coordinate vectors and 1,032 nonzero short relations in the 26 groups. Both lists agree in every group. The minimum lower bound across these 26 groups is at q=50, b=1, 317250915367/82944>0, under the combined correction bound.

At q=33 the former L1 test gave −643381/4096. Keeping the same density and writing s_j as the sum of the three phases in block j, the two conjugate correction sums are A(|s_j|²−3)+2B Re(s_j conjugate(z0)). Since A>0, completing the square gives a lower bound −3A−B²/A for each block. With A=42107/4096, B=40931/4096 and five blocks, the total density numerator is at least 42831862673/172470272>0. Independent enumeration visited 134,245 free vectors, giving 352 nonzero short relations, and all 256 target moments plus normalization were evaluated exactly. A separate exploratory optimization of the polynomial coefficients failed its L1 sufficient test; the original density succeeds using the stronger bound. Failure of a sufficient test is not a counterexample.

## 3. Literature and remaining risk
Reopened Kang arXiv:2609.14463v1, including the forced-coherence lemma and ramp construction; the positive classes are primes, twice primes, and powers of two. The unclassified cases are explicitly left undecided there. Rechecked Lenstra's survey of Conway–Jones's signed rational short-relation bound, and searched related vanishing-sum classifications and uniform-average-mixing work. No matching full positive theorem was found in this bounded search. Related unit-root tools are classical and not claimed as new.
