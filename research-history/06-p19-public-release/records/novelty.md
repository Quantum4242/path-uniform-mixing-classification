# Targeted novelty check — 2026-11-27

The specific question is existence of a Borel probability law on readout times giving J/19 for the adjacency quantum walk on P19, with a stronger explicit construction on integer times. It is not instantaneous or approximate instantaneous mixing, nor the usual infinite-time Cesàro average.

## Verified primary source

- Musung Kang, *Arithmetic of Bohr Frequencies Governs Uniform Mixing in Randomly Timed Quantum Spin Chains*, arXiv:2609.14463v1, 13 September 2026. [Abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.14463), [full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.14463v1). The theorem and Outlook explicitly leave moduli outside its existence and nonexistence families undecided by that work. Its equation (4) gives the coherence target used here. At q=20, neither positive criterion (prime, twice prime, power of two) nor negative criterion (divisible by 15 or 21, or divisible by 6 with q>=12) applies. The present manuscript does not claim that the target-coherence lemma or the relative-interior technique originates here.
- Baptista, Coutinho and Marques, [Unexpected Averages of Mixing Matrices](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.16378) (2023 preprint), background on random-time averaging.
- Godsil, [Average mixing of continuous quantum walks](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcta.2013.05.006) (2013), path spectral average formula.
